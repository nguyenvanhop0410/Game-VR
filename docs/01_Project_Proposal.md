# De tai: VR Color Circle phan loai mau theo cap do (Three.js + WebXR)

## 1. Muc tieu
- Xay dung ung dung VR chay tren trinh duyet, nhan dien va phan loai mau.
- Tang tinh tuong tac va phan xa thong qua gameplay nhat-tha mau trong khong gian 3D.

## 2. Noi dung chinh
- Level 1: Mau co ban (Primary)
- Level 2: Mau thu cap (Secondary)
- Level 3: Mau bac ba (Tertiary)

Trong moi level, nguoi choi nhat bi mau va dat vao dung vi tri tren vong tron mau.

## 3. Luat choi
- Dung toan bo mau trong level hien tai -> mo khoa level tiep theo.
- Hoan thanh level 3 -> chien thang.

## 4. Che do choi
- Easy: khong gioi han thoi gian.
- Hard: gioi han thoi gian ngan.

## 5. Nen tang va cong nghe
- Frontend: Three.js
- VR: WebXR API (VRButton + renderer.xr)
- Build tool: Vite

## 6. Yeu cau ky thuat
- Co am thanh nen.
- Co hieu ung am thanh khi thao tac.
- Toi uu hieu nang de chay muot trong moi truong VR.
